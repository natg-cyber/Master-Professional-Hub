# Product overview

## Purpose

Master Professional Hub is a bilingual personal career-coaching workspace. It connects job search, application preparation, interview readiness, and networking while preserving the user's control over decisions and external actions.

## User

One professional who wants coaching aligned to a stated sector, target roles, location (when relevant), and career goals. The product must reuse a current professional profile rather than asking for it repeatedly.

## Product principles

- **Evidence first:** Never invent experience, outcomes, skills, tools, credentials, or personal details.
- **One intake per opportunity:** Store job context once and reuse it through the application phases.
- **Networking stands alone:** A contact or conversation never implies job seeking, an application, or a promise of help.
- **Optional connection:** Link networking records with an opportunity only at the user's direction.
- **Human approval:** Show edits before applying them; never apply, message, or contact automatically.
- **Portable data:** Preserve the supplied tracker structure and support downloads.
- **Honest capability boundaries:** Do not claim search, AI, persistence, or integrations unless implemented.

## Methodologies

The product makes its working methods explicit: collaborative career coaching, Google XYZ for evidence-based resume bullets, senior recruiter first-pass reading for potential red flags, and START for behavioral interview stories. See [methodologies](methodologies.md) for definitions and safeguards.

## MVP boundary

The app build prompt defines a local-first, single-user MVP with opportunities, contacts, conversations, optional links, workflow states, approvals, and import/export. Employer search, external messaging, cloud sync, and AI API connections are later integrations.
